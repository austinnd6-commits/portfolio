Fixing DKIM for a one-person business

Deliverability

Problem. A customer's DKIM and SPF showed as "Fix" in the platform. They used Google Workspace and had no IT team or DNS experience. Several agents had already sent them back to their mail provider, and they were frustrated.

What I did. Our usual process is to point customers to Google for the records, but I went further. I ran a live Zoom call and walked them through finding the DKIM key in Google, adding it to their DNS, and turning on "Start authentication" so Google signs their emails.

Result. The customer can now repeat the process on their own.

Ticket escalated → Live Zoom call → DKIM key from Google → Added to DNS → Start authentication

Escalating with evidence, from ticket to root cause

Escalations

Problem. Integration and API issues, such as Salesforce, HubSpot or Pipedrive sync failures and permission errors, sometimes need engineering.

What I did. Reproduce the issue with REST APIs, Postman and webhooks, write the escalation in Jira Service Management with clear reproduction steps, and stay on the case with Core Product and Engineering until the root cause is fixed.

Reproduce in Postman → Jira escalation → Engineering → Root-cause fix

What I did. Used resolved tickets and call recordings to correct its answers.

Tickets and recordings → Find wrong answers → Correct the bot
